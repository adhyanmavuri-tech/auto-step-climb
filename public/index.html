Steps

As in the MCDU: WAYPOINT/FL, one per line.

Some OFPs step down for airspace level rules. Off: the aircraft stays at the higher level and the climbs back up are skipped.

Asks BeyondATC for each step and climbs only once ATC clears it. BeyondATC has to be running on this PC. Turn on Auto Respond in BeyondATC so it reads ATC back for you.

Off (the safe setting): a step BeyondATC does not clear is skipped, so the aircraft never climbs without a clearance. On: when BeyondATC is not running, does not answer, or refuses twice, the step is flown anyway. It only matters with Live control on. Leave it off on VATSIM or IVAO.

For any aircraft the app does not control, such as the iniBuilds A340 and A350, the PMDG 777 and 737, the Fenix A320 family, the FlyByWire A32NX and A380X, the Headwind A339X and the iniBuilds A320neo and A321LR. Before each step point (about 10 NM ahead) the app asks BeyondATC for the new level and tells you the answer. The aircraft climbs by itself, or you climb it. The app sends nothing to the aircraft. BeyondATC has to be running on this PC.


      
Waypoints are looked up on your SimBrief route. No OFP? Use +NM/FL, for example +300/370 for 300 NM after level-off; each later +NM/FL counts from the end of the step before it. The box starts filled with the OFP's steps.
